About CLOUDS Club

CLOUDS (Computer Leaders Official United Development Society) is the official student association of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Sahyadri College of Engineering & Management, Mangaluru. The association hosts flagships like the ARPAN charity techno-cultural fest.

Vision

To nurture competent computer science professionals with technical excellence, innovation capabilities, and strong ethical values to meet global industry challenges and societal demands.

Mission

  • Provide effective engineering education through collaborative industry-academia interactions to address real-world demands.
  • Empower prospective computer science engineers with exceptional leadership abilities, organizational teamwork, and robust professional ethics.
  • Cultivate a sustainable culture of technical innovation, continuous learning, and active research development within the student community.
